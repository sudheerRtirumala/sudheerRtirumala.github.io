About

I am a power electronics engineer with 24 years of experience taking power-conversion systems from concept to high-volume production across electric-vehicle charging, solar and storage inverters, data-center power, uninterruptible power supplies, and consumer products. My work spans wide-bandgap (SiC, GaN, B-TRAN™) converter design, digital control, planar magnetics, thermal engineering, and grid-interactive systems.

I am currently Chief Engineer for Bi-Directional EV Charging & V2X products at Emporia Energy, where I lead the design of a 12 kW bidirectional EV charger with vehicle-to-home and vehicle-to-grid capability, and a PhD researcher at the University of Colorado studying small-signal stability and control interactions in converter-dominated power systems, including grid-following and grid-forming converters.
